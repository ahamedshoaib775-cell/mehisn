User Data Deletion Instructions

InstaDM Automation | Instagram Graph API Data Erasure Guidelines

Method 1: Revoke Access via Instagram (Immediate)

Under Meta Developer Policy, you can revoke application permissions at any time directly through Instagram settings:

  1. Open Instagram app or visit Instagram.com.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy > Website Permissions > Apps and Websites.
  3. Under Active, locate InstaDM Automation and click Remove.

Revoking access immediately invalidates all access tokens and stops all webhook event processing.

Method 2: Request Full Database Erasure

To purge all historic logs, account records, and stored configuration from our database:

Email our privacy team at: privacy@instadm.app

Please include your Instagram Handle / Username. All matching records will be permanently erased within 48 hours and a confirmation email will be issued.

Data Retention Policy

  • Webhook comment logs are automatically purged after 30 days.
  • Access tokens are deleted immediately upon account disconnection.
  • No full DM thread conversations or personal media content are stored long-term.
