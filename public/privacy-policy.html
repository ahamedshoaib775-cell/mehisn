1. Overview & Scope

InstaDM Automation ("we", "our", or "the Service") provides Instagram comment-to-DM marketing automation tools for Instagram Business and Creator accounts. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data in full compliance with Meta Platform Terms and Instagram Graph API policies.

2. Information We Collect via Meta API

When you authorize our application via Meta OAuth, we access the following scoped permissions:

  • Instagram Account Profile: User ID, username, profile image URL, account type, and follower count.
  • Media & Posts: Media IDs, captions, permalinks, and publication timestamps for posts enabled for automation.
  • Comment Webhook Events: Comment text, comment IDs, commenter usernames, and scoped commenter IDs to detect keywords (e.g., "LOCATION").
  • Access Tokens: Encrypted access tokens required to perform API requests (direct messages and public replies).

3. How We Use Information

Data is used exclusively to deliver the automated workflow services:

  • Dispatching Instagram Direct Messages containing requested links or location details.
  • Posting optional public comment replies (e.g. "Sent to your DMs!").
  • Maintaining execution logs and analytics within your dashboard.

4. Data Sharing & Privacy

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or share your data with third-party advertisers.

Data is transmitted securely to Meta Platform endpoints for execution and stored in encrypted Supabase database infrastructure.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion Instructions

You can revoke access or delete your data at any time:

Option A: Revoke via Instagram App

  1. Open Instagram > Settings & Privacy > Website Permissions > Apps and Websites.
  2. Locate InstaDM Automation and click Remove.

Option B: Request Database Erasure

Email privacy@instadm.app with your Instagram username. All stored records, logs, and tokens will be permanently deleted within 48 hours.

6. Security & Contact

We enforce TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit and AES-256 for data at rest.

For questions or support, contact: privacy@instadm.app
